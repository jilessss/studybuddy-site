Study Buddy

A study companion for criminology students.

What it is

Study Buddy is a mobile app built as a capstone project to help criminology students review more effectively. Students sign in with their school Google (GSuite) account.

What you can do

  • Take a diagnostic quiz and get a personalized study plan
  • Practice with mock tests and flashcards
  • Read reviewer-style study notes
  • Plan with a calendar, task manager and Pomodoro timer
  • Keep personal notes and earn achievement badges

Sign-in

Study Buddy uses "Continue with GSuite Account" for sign-in. Only school accounts ending in @g.batstate-u.edu.ph are accepted. We only read your name and email address from your Google account.
