Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Study Buddy ("the app") is a student capstone project that helps criminology students study. This policy explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Account information: when you sign in with your school Google account, we receive your name and email address. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Profile details you enter: such as your section, year level, SR code and learning preference.
  • Study data you create in the app: quiz and mock test results, notes, tasks, calendar events, Pomodoro sessions and badges.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To show your progress and save your notes, tasks and schedule.
  • To generate a personalized study plan. Your diagnostic quiz results are sent to an AI service (Google Gemini) for this purpose.

Sign-in restrictions

Only school accounts ending in @g.batstate-u.edu.ph can use the app. Other accounts are signed out automatically.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored with Supabase, our database and authentication provider. Access is limited by account, so you can only see your own study data. Administrators of the app can view student profiles and progress for school-management purposes.

Sharing

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising. It is shared only with the service providers needed to run the app (Supabase for storage and sign-in, and Google for sign-in and the AI study plan).

Your choices

You can edit your profile in Account Settings. To have your account and data deleted, contact us at the email below.

Changes

We may update this policy as the project develops. The date above shows the latest version.
